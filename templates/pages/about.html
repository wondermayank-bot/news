Where our stories come from

We follow official websites such as CBSE, NTA, UGC, PIB, SSC, IBPS, RBI, MPESB and MPPSC, and trusted news publishers such as The Hindu, BBC News and The Indian Express. Every story names its source and links to the original report or official notice. We do not do original on-the-ground reporting. We summarise what our sources publish, in plain English.

Editorial policy

  • Every story names and links its source, so you can read the full original.
  • We stick to what the source says. We avoid opinion, speculation and clickbait headlines.
  • For exam and job updates, we point you to the official notice. Always confirm dates, eligibility and fees on the official website.
  • When we get something wrong, we correct it. See the corrections policy.
  • Publishers and authorities can ask us to change or remove a story at any time.
