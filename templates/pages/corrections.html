Our commitment

If a story on {{siteName}} is wrong or misleading, we correct it. We would rather fix a mistake quickly than leave it standing.

How to report a mistake

Email {{email}} with:

  • the link to the story,
  • what is wrong,
  • a link to the official or original source that shows the right information, if you have one.

What we do

  • We check the story against its source and the official website.
  • If it is wrong, we correct the story, or remove it if it cannot be fixed.
  • For a significant change, we add a note to the story and list it below.
  • We reply to the person who reported it as soon as we can, usually within a few days.

Removal requests

Publishers, authorities and individuals can ask us to change or remove a story. Email the same address and tell us which story and why. We take these requests seriously and act on them promptly.
