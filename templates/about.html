About ThunderStudy News

A daily news briefing in plain English for students and curious readers in India.

What is ThunderStudy News?

ThunderStudy News is a free news blog from ThunderStudy, the exam-prep project by Wondermayank. It covers India, world, business, technology, education, science and sports, and publishes a fresh batch of stories every day at {{publishTime}}.

How stories are made

An automated pipeline reads headlines and summaries from public news feeds and news APIs. An AI model then writes a short, original brief in plain English: a quick answer, key points, a few short paragraphs and a small FAQ. Each brief links to the publisher it is based on.

The briefs are AI-assisted summaries, not original reporting. We do not have reporters on the ground, and a brief only contains what the source summary told us. For the full story, always open the original report linked on each page.

Editorial policy

  • Every story names and links its source publisher.
  • The AI is instructed to use only facts from the source, to avoid opinion, and to leave details out rather than guess.
  • Stories are labelled as AI-assisted at the top and bottom of the page.
  • We do not publish paid or sponsored news.
  • Publishers can ask us to remove or change a brief at any time.

Corrections and contact

If you spot a mistake, tell us through the contact page and include the link to the story. We will check it and correct or remove the brief.

Follow and reuse

Subscribe with the RSS feed or the JSON feed. A machine-readable site summary for AI assistants and search engines is available in llms.txt. When you cite a story, please link to its page.
